💰 FinanceHub

A single-file, offline-first personal finance web app built for Indian users. 🇮🇳 No backend, no build step, no dependencies — just one HTML file that runs entirely in your browser, with 100% of your data staying on your own device. 🔒

Track your monthly budget 📊, manage investments (EPFO, PPF, APY, Stocks) 📈, plan your retirement 🧓, and get insights into your financial health — complete with a voice-guided tour 🎤, dark/light/terminal themes 🌗, and a privacy mode 🕶️ that blurs your numbers until you tap them.

🔗 Live demo: pratikjain12319.github.io/FinanceHub


🚀 How It Works

1️⃣ Budget Tab 📊
Add and edit your own custom expense rows — fully flexible, not locked to preset categories ✏️
Enter your salary and expenses, and FinanceHub instantly calculates your surplus 💵
A color-coded health bar tells you at a glance whether your surplus is Good 🟢, Warning 🟡, Bad 🔴, or Zero ⚪
Save a snapshot of any month to build a history you can revisit later 🕰️
Export your budget as a CSV 📄 or PDF 🖨️, or generate a ready-to-send email report ✉️

2️⃣ Invest Tab 📈
Track all your investments in one place: EPFO, PPF, APY, Stocks, and Other 🏦
Log manual SIP contributions each month, with a smart lock so you can't accidentally double-enter the same month 🔒
Every SIP update automatically creates a restore point in your Financial Ledger — so you can roll back if you make a mistake ⏪
Edit, delete, or clear ledger entries anytime 🗂️

3️⃣ Retire Tab 🧓
See a year-by-year retirement projection using proper EPFO methodology 📐
Plan your PPF growth with a dedicated calculator 💹
Visualize everything with Chart.js graphs 📉📈, switchable between table and chart view 🔄
Export your full retirement projection as a PDF 🖨️

4️⃣ Insights Tab 🔍
Get a Financial Health Score based on your savings rate and spending mix 🩺
View your "Expense DNA" — a visual breakdown of where your money actually goes 🧬
Run the Salary Hike Simulator to see how a raise would affect your savings and goals 🎉
Set Goal Buckets (e.g., a trip, gadget, emergency fund), top them up, and track progress toward each 🎯
Keep an eye on your Net Worth Tracker and surplus sparkline trend 📊

5️⃣ Extra Features ✨
🎤 Guided Voice Tour — a built-in narrator (using the Web Speech API) walks new users through every tab, step by step
🌗 Three Themes — Light, Dark, and a retro green-on-black Terminal mode
🕶️ Privacy Mode — blurs sensitive numbers until you tap/focus on them, handy for screen sharing
📱 Mobile-first & responsive — works great on phone, tablet, or desktop
💾 100% local storage — your data never leaves your device, no sign-up or account needed

🛠️ Tech Stack
HTML • CSS • Vanilla JavaScript • Bootstrap 5 • Chart.js 4 • Web Speech API • localStorage

⚡ Getting Started
Clone or download this repository 📥
Open index.html directly in any modern browser — no server or build step required 🌐
(Optional) Host it for free on GitHub Pages for access from any device 🚀

🔐 Data & Privacy
All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to a server 🛡️. Clearing your browser data or switching devices/browsers will reset the app — so use the CSV/PDF export options to back up your data periodically 💾.
